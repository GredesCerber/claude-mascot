# Medium and complex art-pack actions

Owned implementation: `rust/src/pack_actions/advanced.rs` only. No commits or pushes.

The original three contact sheets (`work/idea-pack/contact-{0,1,2}.png`) were inspected. The implementation authors new procedural props in whole logical cells and retains the recognizable object concept of each source. Bodies, faces, fingers, other people and other pets are never painted by this module. Scene 26 deliberately uses one Claude at one laptop; genuine pet-to-pet programming remains owned by the social system.

Each action has 12 discrete timeline stages derived from its own catalog duration. Stages 0 and 11 use neutral body and no props. Stage 1 prepares/reaches, stages 2-9 perform the individual action, stage 10 lowers hands and removes props. Completed towers, pins and marks remain complete during cleanup; their opacity steps from 170 to 85 to 0. Laptops close before removal. This is authored pose stepping, with no sine movement or random category effect.

## Geometry and layering

All props use the common `cell` helper, exact facing mirror and common FEET anchor. Physical palm coordinates are `body_x + hand.x`, `bob + hand.y`. The barbell uses the same height function for both palms and shaft. Its floor pose has plate bottoms exactly on y=0; no unsupported leg pair is lifted during the exercise. Left-facing art is a whole-cell mirror, including hands and tools.

`draw` goes below the canonical body. Readable panels are placed beside or above it. Laptop lids show a matte exterior because their screens face Claude. `draw_foreground` adds only the thin costume edge or ninja headband edge; it never paints a face. Root owns the arm connector from canonical shoulder to authored palm.

## Verification and limits

`rustfmt --edition 2021 rust/src/pack_actions/advanced.rs` passed. Five module tests cover ID ownership and neutral boundaries, all 52 scenes mirrored at 12 stages with the support-plane/bounds assertions, exact two-palm barbell grip heights, object-state changes at constant full opacity followed by cleanup, and preservation of completed tower/map geometry during cleanup. Root runs Cargo checks centrally; this worker has not run a competing full test process.

The illustration pack supplies one frame per concept rather than animation source. These are original procedural interpretations with an intentionally compact twelve-stage vocabulary. The artwork is not a pixel-for-pixel tracing; debugging, monitoring, connection and check marks are cosmetic animations, with no real system operation. The three laptop scenes share a mechanical laptop primitive while their action content differs. Source scene 26's additional character is deliberately omitted. Rocket departure ends in a stepped fade within the render bounds, because the desktop window and physics position must not move for this cosmetic action.
